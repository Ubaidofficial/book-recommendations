## Email template

Subject: Photo for your BookMentions page

Hi {first name},

BookMentions (bookmentions.net) lists the books people recommend, each linked to where they said it. You have a page at https://bookmentions.net/people/{slug} with {N} recommendations.

We would like to show a headshot on it. Would you be happy for us to use a photo of you, and how would you like it credited? You can reply with a link to an official press photo, or attach one. We will display it only with your OK, with the credit you choose, and will remove it on request.

Thank you,
{name}, BookMentions

## When a yes arrives

Record the person, the photo URL, the credit line they asked for, and a link or screenshot of the reply. Store the credit line in `avatar_attribution`, `avatar_license` as "Used with permission", and `avatar_source_url` as the page it came from.
